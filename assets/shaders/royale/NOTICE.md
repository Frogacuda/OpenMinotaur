# CRT Royale notices

CRT Royale is copyright 2014 TroggleMonkey and is distributed under GPL version
2 or later, as stated in its shader headers. Original source-level attribution,
converted shader notices, the GPL text and upstream acknowledgments are retained.
The included NTSC shader files have no additional attribution header.

The shader sources, phosphor-mask textures and preset retain their original
contents. provenance.json records upstream-relative names and checksums.
The host selects flat geometry and supplies OpenGL/OpenGL ES compatibility
headers without replacing the filter kernels. Scanlines and masks follow the
virtual monitor axes independently of game orientation.
